# Unspoken Pilot Program: Clinical Oversight Proposal

## Executive Summary
We are seeking 3 licensed clinicians (LMFT, LCSW, or PsyD) to oversee a **Closed Beta Pilot** of 50 users. The goal is to validate "Unspoken" not as a replacement for therapy, but as a "Force Multiplier" for between-session support.

## The Problem: "The Weekly Gap"
Therapy happens once a week. Crises happen daily.
*   **Client Reality**: 167 hours per week are spent alone.
*   **Therapist Reality**: No visibility into client mood between sessions.

## The Solution: The "Unspoken Dashboard"
Participating clinicians will receive access to a HIPAA-compliant dashboard showing aggregate biometric data for their consented clients.

### Features
1.  **Red Flag Utility**:
    *   *System*: "Client A showed High Voice Jitter (0.8) on Thursday at 2 AM."
    *   *Action*: Therapist knows to ask about Thursday night in the next session.
2.  **Truth Calibration**:
    *   *Client Report*: "I had a good week."
    *   *Unspoken Data*: "Average Facial Valence was -0.4 (Sad)."
    *   *Outcome*: Deeper conversation about masking.

## Pilot Structure
*   **Duration**: 4 Weeks.
*   **Cohort**: 50 Users (Low-risk, anxiety/mild depression).
*   **Protocol**:
    *   Users chat with Unspoken daily.
    *   Therapists review "Weekly Biometric Summaries."
*   **Success Metric**:
    *   Therapist Qualitative Score: "Did the data help you treat the patient better?"

## Safety & Ethics
*   **No Diagnosis**: The AI never labels conditions.
*   **Crisis Hand-off**: Immediate redirection to the partnering clinician's emergency contract or 988.
