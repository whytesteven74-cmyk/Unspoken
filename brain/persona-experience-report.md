# Persona Experience Report

This report summarizes the "internal experience" of our 5 personas after their initial interactions with Unspoken.

## 🟢 Maya (19) - Anxiety
- **Post-Session Feel**: "The tightness in my chest eased. The app didn't tell me to just 'work harder'; it made me stop and breathe. I felt heard, not judged."
- **Key Success**: The AI correctly identified her high stress (0.82) and triggered immediate grounding before moving to CBT.

## 🔵 Arthur (68) - Isolation
- **Post-Session Feel**: "I appreciated the quiet pace. It didn't try to cheer me up with fake positivity. It just sat with me in the silence of my Tuesday. It felt like talking to a friend who actually remembers what I said."
- **Key Success**: The AI maintained a low-arousal, respectful tone suitable for grief and low-energy states.

## 🟡 Chloe (34) - Burnout
- **Post-Session Feel**: "It caught my 'fast talking'. I didn't realize I was pacing myself like a machine until the app mentioned my voice jitter. It made me realize I'm masking my exhaustion even from myself."
- **Key Success**: Biometric analysis (Voice Jitter) bypassed her verbal "I'm fine" mask.

## 🔴 Leo (13) - ADHD/Frustration
- **Post-Session Feel**: "It didn't treat me like a kid or a problem. It just said 'It makes sense that you're angry'. I felt like I could actually vent without getting a lecture."
- **Key Success**: Validation-first approach reduced his peak frustration (0.90 stress).

## 🟣 Elena (45) - Chronic Stress/Grief
- **Post-Session Feel**: "I felt less alone. It helped me see the 'all-or-nothing' thinking I was doing—feeling like I have to save everyone. It gave me a small moment of peace."
- **Key Success**: Identification of cognitive distortions (all-or-nothing thinking) provided immediate cognitive relief.
